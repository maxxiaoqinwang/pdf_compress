# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is one person reading EPUB files from a phone, often inside WeChat's built-in browser. They want a quick private reader for novels and image-heavy EPUBs without publishing a mini program or uploading books to a server.

## Product Purpose

This product opens a user-selected EPUB in the browser and makes it comfortable to read on mobile. Success means the reader feels natural inside WeChat: minimal chrome, one-handed controls, local file handling, and no stored book library.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, private, readable. The interface should feel like a focused reading tool, not a demo page or desktop app squeezed onto a phone.

## Anti-references

Avoid desktop-style permanent sidebars on mobile, large top toolbars, English labels for everyday controls, marketing-page hero sections during reading, and controls that require two hands.

## Design Principles

1. Reading owns the screen.
2. Mobile interaction comes first.
3. Controls appear where the thumb can reach them.
4. Privacy stays visible in the product choices, not as decoration.
5. Text novels and image-heavy books need different reading defaults.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast themes, visible focus states, touch targets large enough for mobile, reduced motion-friendly transitions, and user-controlled font size and line height.
